Privacy Policy

Sanatanam · Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Sanatanam ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application Sanatanam (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Location Data

  • Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • Purpose: We request location access to calculate precise, location-specific Panchang details, Hindu calendar events, Sunrise/Sunset timings, and Muhurtas based on your current geographical coordinates. Location data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

B. Personal & Account Information

  • Authentication: When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we collect basic profile information, including your Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture.
  • Purpose: To manage your user profile, sync your preferences, and maintain saved mantra counts or settings across devices.

C. Device & Usage Information

  • Push Notifications: We collect Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens to deliver daily notifications, Panchang updates, and reminders.
  • Local Storage: Your app settings and offline preferences are securely stored locally on your device using Room Database and encrypted storage.

2. Third-Party Services

The App integrates trusted third-party services that may process data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Authentication & Messaging: Firebase Privacy & Security

3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active.

How to Request Data / Account Deletion

  1. Sending an email to hunternineone@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Including your registered email address.

For deletion requests sent by email, we will permanently delete your account details and stored cloud preferences within 30 days. Online deletion is processed after Google verification and your confirmation.

You can also delete your Sanatanam account online. This securely removes account information and user-created records stored on our servers.

4. Children’s Privacy

Sanatanam does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can immediately delete the information.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational precautions (including HTTPS encryption and secure database controls) to prevent the loss, misuse, or alteration of your personal data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
